# College_accounting_system
1. Core Accounting and Financial Management Features

Ensure that the system includes all essential accounting functionalities, such as:

Student account and financial record management.
Tuition fee and registration fee management.
Invoice generation and management.
Payment recording and tracking.
Outstanding balance calculations.
Debtors and creditors management.
Income and expenditure tracking.
General ledger management.
Financial transaction history.
Refund and credit note management.
Budgeting and financial forecasting.
Bank reconciliation.
Financial statements and account summaries.
2. Automated Financial Reporting

One of the key requirements is to introduce an automated reporting system that reduces manual administrative work.

The system should be capable of automatically generating reports such as:

Daily, weekly, and monthly financial reports.
Student fee payment reports.
Outstanding student balance reports.
Revenue and expenditure reports.
Debtors reports.
Payment history reports.
Course and programme revenue reports.
Financial performance summaries.
Reports should be downloadable in formats such as PDF and Excel, where applicable.

3. Automated Student Payment Reminders and Email Notifications

I would particularly like this feature to be prioritised.

The accounting system should automatically identify students with outstanding balances and send them email notifications reminding them of their financial obligations.

The functionality should include:

Automatic identification of students with outstanding balances.
Automated email reminders for unpaid fees.
Customisable email templates.
Notifications indicating the outstanding amount and payment deadline.
Scheduled reminders (e.g. weekly or monthly).
Confirmation emails when payments have been received.
Escalation notifications for significantly overdue accounts, where appropriate.
A record of all emails and notifications sent to students.
The system should also allow authorised finance personnel to manually send reminders to individual students or selected groups.

4. Financial Dashboards and Analytics

Develop a centralised financial dashboard that provides an overview of the institution's financial position.

The dashboard should display:

Total revenue collected.
Total outstanding student fees.
Number of students with outstanding balances.
Total payments received.
Monthly income trends.
Outstanding debtors.
Financial performance indicators.
The information should update automatically as financial transactions are recorded.

5. User Roles, Permissions and Security

Ensure that the system supports different levels of access for finance administrators, finance officers, management, and other authorised personnel.

Financial information must be protected through appropriate access controls, audit trails, transaction logs, and secure handling of student financial records.

6. Integration and Automation

Please also assess opportunities to integrate the accounting system with other institutional platforms, particularly:

The Student Management System.
The Student Registration System.
The Learning Management System (LMS).
Payment gateways and banking platforms.
The institutional email notification system.
The goal is to minimise duplicate data entry and ensure that student financial information remains consistent across all platforms.
